A difficult moment, made clearer

When a leaf changes, the next decision matters.

Crop health rarely comes down to a photograph alone. The crop, the weather and what's happening nearby all shape what a farmer should do next.

FIELD OBSERVATION · THE FIRST SIGNALS MATTER

Symptoms can arrive lateVisible damage may be a sign to look more closely across the field.
02
Good advice isn't always close byGetting a knowledgeable second opinion can take time and travel.
03
Conditions change the riskHumidity, rain and growth stage can shift what deserves attention.
04
Treatment without clarity costsActing on a guess can waste inputs and put the crop under more stress.
The Fieldnote pathway

Four steps from a concern to a community signal.

A joined-up workflow that keeps the farmer in control and brings a human expert into the picture when it counts.

01 / OBSERVE

DETECT

Share a close photograph and tell us which crop and stage you're seeing.

02 / UNDERSTAND

PREDICT

Consider weather and local reports to understand what may need attention.

03 / CHECK

VERIFY

Send uncertain or higher-risk cases for an agricultural expert to review.

04 / SHARE

ALERT

Verified signals help nearby farmers stay aware of emerging concerns.

Our mission

Help good decisions travel further.

Fieldnote is a concept for a more connected crop-health ecosystem: one that helps make agricultural expertise easier to reach and early intervention easier to consider.

→Earlier awareness of crop-health concerns
→Better-informed conversations about treatment
→Less uncertainty about what to check next
